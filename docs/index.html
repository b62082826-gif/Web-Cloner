    What it does

    One link in, a working copy out.

    WebGrabber reads the page, finds every file it needs, downloads them side by side and rewrites the links so nothing points back to the internet.

    Many files at once

    Pages, stylesheets, scripts, fonts and images download in parallel, so a large page finishes in seconds. Anything that fails keeps its live link instead of breaking the page.

    How it works

    Three steps, in this order.

    1. Paste the address

      Type example.com or paste a full link. The bar under the field fills when the link looks valid.

    2. Choose a folder

      Pick where copies go. WebGrabber remembers it, and your recent sites, for next time.

    3. Press Download

      Watch it run, then open the copy in your browser or jump straight to the folder.
