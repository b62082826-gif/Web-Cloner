What it does

One link in, a working copy out.

It reads the page, finds every file it needs, downloads them side by side and rewrites the links so nothing points back to the internet.

Parallel downloads

Files are fetched several at a time, so a large page finishes in seconds instead of minutes.

CSS that still works

Fonts and background images referenced inside stylesheets are downloaded and their URLs are rewritten.

Live progress

A big percentage, an elapsed timer and a log of every file, with a Cancel button that cleans up after itself.

Two ways to save

Pick the shape of the copy.

How it works

Three steps, in this order.

  1. Paste the address

    Type example.com or paste a full link. The bar under the field fills when the link looks valid.

  2. Choose a folder

    Pick where copies go. WebGrabber remembers it and your recent sites for next time.

  3. Press Download

    Watch it run, then open the copy in your browser or jump straight to the folder.
